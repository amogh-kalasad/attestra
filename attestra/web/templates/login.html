Attestra

From findings to signed attestation.

Attestra turns security testing results into audit-ready evidence: import your scanners' results, validate and risk-rank findings, map them to framework controls, and issue hashed, four-eyes approved, signed reports. It never runs attacks itself.

The workflow

  1. 01
    Signed authorizationHashed and write-once. Imports are refused without a valid one.
  2. 02
    Targets in scopeAssets with criticality 1–5, internet/internal exposure, PCI CDE flag.
  3. 03
    Test planScope and approach for the VA / PT test window.
  4. 04
    Automated scansZAP, Nuclei, Trivy, Semgrep, Gitleaks, Checkov, testssl and more.
  5. 05
    Results importedDe-duplicated by fingerprint and risk-ranked with CVSS, EPSS and CISA KEV.
  6. 06
    Signed reportsVersioned, SHA-256 hashed, four-eyes approved, then signed.
